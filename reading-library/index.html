CEC Reading Library

CEC Reading Library offers classic stories adapted by CEC English Camp into three reading levels. R1 Access uses simpler vocabulary and shorter sentences, R2 Core is the main grade-level version, and R3 Challenge uses richer vocabulary and more complex language. Choose your grade, then select the reading level that fits you best.

These are CEC educational adaptations for English learners, not the original unabridged texts. Some titles are CEC original works and are labeled separately.
