The Call of the Wild

Grade
Grade 5
Author
Jack London
First published
1903

CEC adaptation based on a public-domain classic, adapted into three reading levels. Original work published 1903, now in the public domain.

Buck is a big, comfortable dog living in California until he is stolen and sold to pull sleds in the frozen Yukon. He must learn hard new rules to survive, and slowly the wild begins to call to him. The story explores loyalty, strength, and nature.
