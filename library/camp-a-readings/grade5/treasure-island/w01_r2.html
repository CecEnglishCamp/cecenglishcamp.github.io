READING 2

🗺️ Treasure Island

Week 01 · Grade 5 · Reading 2

The pirates wanted the treasure. They had a secret plan.

Jim was very scared. He had to warn his friends.

Silver and his men wanted the treasure for themselves. They planned to take the ship by force.

Jim was frightened, but he stayed quiet in the barrel. When the men left, he climbed out carefully.

He ran to tell Doctor Livesey the terrible news. His friends had to know the truth.

As Jim listened from inside the barrel, he understood the whole dangerous plan. Silver and the other pirates wanted to steal the treasure and take control of the ship.

Although Jim was only a boy, he knew that he had to act bravely. Because the men finally moved away, Jim was able to climb out of the barrel without being seen.

He hurried to find Doctor Livesey and Squire Trelawney, whose lives were now in danger. When he told them everything he had heard, they were shocked but grateful. Together, they decided to make a secret plan of their own. The real adventure was about to begin.

Comprehension

내용 확인

1. What did the pirates want to do?

Steal the treasure and take control of the ship.

2. What did Jim do after the men left?

He climbed out of the barrel and ran to warn his friends.

3. Who did Jim tell the news to?

Doctor Livesey and Squire Trelawney.
