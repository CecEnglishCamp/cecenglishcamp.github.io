🗺️ Treasure Island

Week 01 · Grade 5 · Reading 1

One night, Jim was tired. He hid inside the big apple barrel.

Soon he fell asleep. Then he heard voices.

One evening, Jim wanted an apple. He climbed inside the big apple barrel on the deck.

It was warm, and soon he fell asleep. When he woke up, he heard men talking close by.

The voice was Long John Silver, the ship’s cook. Jim stayed very still and listened.

Late one evening, Jim felt hungry and decided to find an apple. He climbed into the large barrel that stood on the deck, not knowing how important this moment would be.

Because the barrel was warm and dark, Jim soon fell fast asleep inside it. However, he woke suddenly when he heard low voices speaking nearby.

The man who was talking turned out to be Long John Silver, the cook whom everyone trusted. As Jim listened, he realized that Silver was not friendly at all. Silver was secretly a pirate, and he was planning something terrible. Jim held his breath and did not make a sound.

Comprehension

내용 확인

1. Where did Jim hide?

He hid inside the big apple barrel.

2. Who was talking near the barrel?

Long John Silver, the ship’s cook.

3. What did Jim learn about Silver?

Silver was secretly a pirate, planning something bad.
