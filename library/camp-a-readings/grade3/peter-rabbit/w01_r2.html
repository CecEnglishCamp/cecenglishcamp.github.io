READING 2

🐰 Peter Rabbit

Week 01 · Grade 3 · Reading 2

Peter goes into the garden. He eats some lettuce and beans. It is very good!

Then he sees Mr. McGregor. Mr. McGregor is very angry. “Stop!” he shouts.

Peter runs and runs. He loses his shoes. He loses his little blue jacket. At last, Peter finds the gate and runs home. Peter is safe, but he is very tired.

Peter goes into the garden. He eats some vegetables.

Then he sees Mr. McGregor. Mr. McGregor is angry.

Peter runs away. He loses his blue jacket. Peter runs home. He is safe now.

Comprehension

내용 확인

1. What does Peter eat in the garden?

He eats some lettuce and beans (vegetables).

2. Who does Peter see?

He sees Mr. McGregor. Mr. McGregor is very angry.

3. What does Peter lose?

He loses his shoes and his little blue jacket.
